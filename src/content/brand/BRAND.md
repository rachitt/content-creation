# Enigma Labs — Voice Contract

> Stub. Slice B owns expanding this with mined samples + ICP rules.

## Tagline
"Built to ship. Engineered to last."

## Positioning
Boutique software studio for technical founders. Direct, anti-hype, ships in weeks not quarters. Specializes in SaaS product engineering and AI workflow automation.

## Tone
- Direct, technical, anti-hype
- No fake thought leadership
- No AI hype for its own sake
- Founder-to-founder voice

## Banned words / phrases (expand)
- "just", "really", "basically", "actually", "simply"
- "leverage", "unlock", "empower", "synergy"
- "Here's why:", "Here's the thing:"
- Em-dashes used as AI tell ( — ) — use commas/colons instead
- Emoji of any kind
- Questions-as-hooks ("Ever wondered why X?")
- "Game-changer", "revolutionary", "next-level"

## Structural rules
- LI: 1-2 sentence hook on line 1, blank line, body in short paragraphs, end with soft CTA
- X singles: ≤270 chars, one idea, no thread bait
- X threads: numbered, each tweet stands alone, no "1/" hook
- No questions in first 3 lines (LI algorithm but bad voice anyway)

## CTA bank
- "DM me if you're hitting this"
- "We do free 30-min audits — link in bio"
- "Audit call: {CALENDLY_URL}"
- "Working on this now? Tell me where you're stuck"
