# ICP — Target customer personas

> Stub. Slice B owns expanding.

Enigma serves technical founders building B2B SaaS or AI workflow products. Common traits:
- 1-10 person team
- $0-5M ARR
- Pre-PMF or post-PMF rebuild
- Frustrated with previous agency engagements
- Ships weekly or wants to

## Verticals
1. Legaltech (contract automation, doc gen, intake)
2. Healthtech (intake, EHR adjacents, patient flow)
3. Fintech (back-office automation, reconciliation)
4. SaaS infra (devtools, observability)
5. AI agency / tooling
6. E-commerce ops automation
7. Insurance ops
8. Real estate ops (PropTech)
9. EdTech (cohort, content ops)
10. B2B services automation (logistics, compliance)

## Pain language (mine more from harvested viral_posts)
- "spent $X on agency, got Figma"
- "MVP in Bubble, now scaling fails"
- "every dev I hire wants to rewrite"
- "AI demo doesn't survive prod"
- "spec drift between sprints"

## Common objections
- "Custom is too expensive" → counter with shipped-in-weeks proof
- "I want to own the IP" → we ship code into your repo
- "How is this different from a freelancer?" → senior team, ops included
